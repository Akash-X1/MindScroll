MindScroll Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

MindScroll is an Android digital wellbeing application designed to help users understand and reduce excessive short-form content scrolling.

Information We Collect

MindScroll does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information with external servers or third parties.

Usage information generated by MindScroll, such as scroll counts, session duration, and activity statistics, is processed and stored locally on the user's device.

Accessibility Service

MindScroll uses the Android Accessibility Service to detect when supported short-form content applications are being used and to identify scrolling activity.

Accessibility information is used only to provide MindScroll's core functionality, including scroll counting, session timing, intervention reminders, and digital wellbeing features.

MindScroll does not use the Accessibility Service to collect passwords, payment information, private messages, or other sensitive personal content. Accessibility data is not transmitted to external servers.

Internet and Data Transmission

MindScroll does not require Internet permission for its core functionality. App activity and usage statistics are processed locally on the device.

Shared Images and Videos

MindScroll may allow users to select or share images or videos with the app for features configured by the user. These files are handled locally on the device and are not uploaded to MindScroll servers.

Third-Party Services

MindScroll does not sell user data or provide collected user information to advertisers or data brokers.

User Control

Users can disable MindScroll's Accessibility Service at any time through Android Accessibility settings. Users may also uninstall MindScroll to remove the application and its locally stored data.

Children's Privacy

MindScroll does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when MindScroll features or privacy practices change. The latest version will be available on this page.
